HOW IT WORKS

From wheat image to useful insight.

WheatVision follows a simple two-stage deep-learning pipeline to analyze an uploaded wheat image.

01

Upload Image

Upload a clear image of wheat grains through the analysis interface.

02

Wheat Detection

The first model checks whether the uploaded image contains wheat.

03

Species Identification

EfficientNetB0 identifies one of the four supported wheat species.

04

Get Insights

View literature-based protein information and food suitability.
